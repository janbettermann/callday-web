# Callday + Zoom

This guide explains how to add, use, and remove the Zoom integration in
Callday. Callday is a cold-calling app for iPhone that moves you through
your call list one lead at a time. When a call turns into a meeting,
Callday handles the follow-up — and if you connect Zoom, it creates the
meeting link for you automatically.

## What the integration does

When you book a meeting after a call and tap **Generate Zoom link**,
Callday creates a scheduled Zoom meeting in **your own Zoom account** for
the date and time you chose, then drops the join link into the
confirmation email and the calendar event. You never switch apps or copy
links by hand. Generating a link is always an explicit tap — nothing is
created without your action.

Each user connects their own Zoom account, and every meeting is created
under that account.

---

## Requirements

- The **Callday app for iPhone** — get it at [callday.io](https://callday.io).
- A **Zoom account** — a free account works. The join link is created in
  your own account, so you stay in control of your meetings.

---

## Adding the integration (connecting Zoom)

1. Open Callday and go to **Settings → Zoom**.
2. Tap **Connect Zoom**.
3. You'll be taken to Zoom's sign-in and consent screen. Review the
   requested permissions and tap **Allow**.
4. You're returned to Callday. Settings → Zoom now shows **Connected**
   with your Zoom email.

That's it — you only connect once.

---

## Using the integration

1. After a call, choose the **Meeting** outcome on the post-call card.
2. Enter the meeting **date and time**.
3. Tap **Generate Zoom link**. Callday creates a scheduled Zoom meeting
   in your account for that time.
4. The join link is added to the **confirmation email** and the
   **calendar event**. You can preview the email before it sends.

If you remove a generated link before booking, the Zoom meeting stays
harmlessly in your account and no link is sent.

---

## Removing the integration (disconnecting Zoom)

You can remove the integration from either side at any time.

**From Callday:**

1. Go to **Settings → Zoom**.
2. Tap **Disconnect**.

This deletes Callday's stored access for your account, so no further Zoom
meetings can be created until you reconnect.

**From Zoom:**

1. Go to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us) and sign in.
2. Open **Manage → Added Apps**.
3. Find **Callday** and click **Remove**.

Removing the app from Zoom revokes Callday's access from Zoom's side.

## Data and permissions

Callday requests only the permissions it needs to create your meetings:

- **View a meeting / create a meeting** — so Callday can schedule the
  Zoom meeting for your booked call.
- **View your user information** (name and email) — shown on the connect
  screen so you can confirm which account is linked.

Your Zoom authorization is stored encrypted and is used only to create
meetings you explicitly request. For full details on what data Callday
processes and how, see our [Privacy Policy](https://callday.io/privacy).
