# Support

Need help with Callday? You're in the right place. We're a small team and
we read every message.

## Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is email:

**[hello@callday.io](mailto:hello@callday.io)**

We typically reply within one business day. To help us help you faster,
include:

- what you were trying to do,
- what happened instead,
- your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).

---

## Common topics

### Zoom integration

Everything about connecting, using, and removing the Zoom integration is
covered in our dedicated guide: [Callday + Zoom](https://callday.io/zoom).
It walks through setup, generating meeting links, and disconnecting from
either side.

### Account and data

For questions about your data — what we store, how to export it, or how
to delete your account — see our
[Privacy Policy](https://callday.io/privacy) or email us and we'll take
care of it.

### Billing

Subscriptions are handled through the Apple App Store. You can manage or
cancel your plan in your iPhone's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
For anything unclear, email us.
